## Daytime plan — wife + Kali (solo days while Rahul is at conference)

- **Denver Children's Museum** — engineered for under-5. Very close to downtown.
- **Denver Botanic Gardens** — photogenic for wife, calm for Kali. Seasonal late-Oct display.

**Wife's agenda:**
- **Cherry Creek North** — this is the luxury shopping district. Lululemon, Hermès, Tiffany, boutiques. Walkable neighborhood. The Denver version of Midtown.

- **16th Street Mall** — big-box/tourist but connects everything for a free bus ride.

## Dinners

**Anchor dinner (book now):**
- **Tavernetta** — upscale Italian, Union Station. James Beard-nominated. Exactly her thing: housemade pasta, great wine list, photogenic interior. Kid-friendly enough for a well-behaved 3-year-old.

**Casual nights:**
- **Work & Class** — Latin-soul comfort food (fried chicken, brisket, rice), tight small space, fun. No reservations, arrive early. **No sushi/Chinese — this is fine, not in that category.**
- **Denver Biscuit Company (RiNo)** — breakfast/brunch with Kali. Massive biscuit sandwiches, casual, kid-beloved.
- **Ultreia (Union Station)** — Spanish pintxos/tapas, lively, walk-in friendly. Good gin/cocktail list. Standby if Tavernetta fills.

## Drinks

**Death & Co Denver (RiNo)** — no-brainer stop. Walk-in, late night. Best cocktail program in Denver: serious old-fashioned game for him, strong gin selection for her. This is the one thing you can't skip.

**Williams & Graham** — speakeasy in a fake bookshelf. Excellent craft cocktails, very small, reservations recommended. Go here for date night if Rahul gets a free evening without Kali.

## Quick read on Denver in late October
- Weather: 50s–60s°F days, 30s at night. Pack layers. Can snow, can also be 65°F. Classic.
- Altitude: 5,280ft. Both of you drink a glass of water first night. Kali will be fine — kids acclimate faster.
- LoDo/Union Station neighborhood is the sweet spot: walkable, stroller-friendly, great food density.
- Halloween weekend (Oct 31): the city goes fun-crazy. Could be great for Kali's first real Halloween in a new city.

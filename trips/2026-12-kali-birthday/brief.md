## Leading option: Quebec City, QC, Canada

### Why it works
- **Old Town (Vieux-Québec)** = UNESCO World Heritage site, essentially a French European city without the transatlantic flight. Cobblestones, 17th-century architecture, Château Frontenac looming over everything.
- **Wife's photography:** One of the most photogenic destinations in North America. The Christmas market + Château Frontenac at dusk = her shot of the year.
- **December timing = magic:** Christmas market at Place D'Youville, horse-drawn calèche rides through the Old Town, lights everywhere. Kali will lose her mind at a horse-drawn carriage in a fairy-tale snow city.
- **French food city:** Excellent bistro/brasserie scene. Not Italian but wife will eat very well. Gin cocktail bars have gotten serious in QC in recent years.
- **Not sushi/Chinese:** Zero conflict.

### The one catch
Cold. December in Quebec City = 20–30°F. Bundle Kali properly and it's fine — the Christmas market is built for it. But if wife or Rahul hate cold, this becomes less fun.

## Flights
- **RDU → YQB (Quebec City Jean Lesage):** Likely connection through YYZ (Toronto) or YUL (Montreal) on Air Canada. ~5–6hr total travel time.
- **Alternative:** RDU → YUL (Montreal, ~2.5hr flight), stay 1 night in Old Montreal, then drive ~3hr to Quebec City. Adds a night but Montreal Old Port is also beautiful + it's a smoother routing.

## Hotel

**No Marriott Explore rate in Vieux-Québec.** Two options:

| Option | Vibe | Approx. Cost |
|--------|------|-------------|
| **Fairmont Le Château Frontenac** | The full splurge — 1893 iconic castle above the St. Lawrence. Birthday trip energy. | ~$400–700 CAD/night (≈$300–500 USD) |
| **Boutique hotel in Vieux-Québec** (Hôtel Clarendon, Auberge Saint-Antoine) | Smart-luxury pick, walking distance to everything | ~$200–350 CAD/night |

**Read:** Château Frontenac IS the trip for a 3-year-old's birthday. She'll think she's in a real castle. The price is high but so was Atlantis — if this is the birthday trip, go Château Frontenac and don't look back. Auberge Saint-Antoine is the smart-luxury runner-up (on the waterfront, 5-min walk to Frontenac).

## What to do

**With Kali:**
- Horse-drawn calèche ride through Old Town — she'll never forget it
- Christmas Market at Place D'Youville (usually opens late November)
- Plains of Abraham in the snow — wide open, she can run, photogenic backdrop
- Ice sculptures (if mid/late December, some installations start early)
- Musée de la Civilisation — kid-friendly exhibits, good rainy-day backup

**For wife:**
- Old Town walking photos: Rue du Trésor, Quartier Petit-Champlain (most photogenic street in Canada), cliff staircases
- Shopping: Quartier Petit-Champlain boutiques (arts, crafts, Canadian designers — not luxury labels, but interesting)
- Fairmont Spa day if budget allows

**Dinner anchor:**
- **Laurie Raphaël** — best fine dining in QC City. French-influenced tasting menu, photogenic plating. Pre-book.
- **Le 48 Saint-Paul** — Old Port brasserie, very French, more casual. Good for a family night with Kali.
- **Cochon Dingue** — beloved local brasserie. Kali-friendly, great comfort French food.

---

## Timing considerations
- **Early December (Dec 5–10):** Christmas market is open, not yet peak-busy. Easier travel. Works if Kali's birthday is early December.
- **Mid-December (Dec 12–17):** Sweet spot — fully decorated, not yet school-break madness.
- **Dec 18+:** School breaks start → busier + pricier everywhere.
